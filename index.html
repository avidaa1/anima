Meet Vida, waving beside IO with rainbow curly hair - she laughs on her own every so often, and cracks up with a little jump whenever IO gets zapped. Tap her to turn her into a small white bunny with a rainbow tail and rainbow eyes, or drag and throw her - she shatters and rebuilds too, laughing when she lands. Touch either of them to light up an '80s synthwave background - neon grid, retro sun, laser scanlines. Tap "Hood" to push IO's hood down and reveal slowly shifting rainbow hair, or tap "Turn" to see him in side profile. Move the neon pointer close to whichever one is nearer to zap them - pick Shock, Heat, Magical, or Kryptonite up top. Tap "Moves" to open IO's gesture menu. Click IO's body to poke it (it waves back), or click the feet to make it jump. Drag and throw it - it shatters and reforms, and passes through the screen edges. Shake it (drag it rapidly back and forth) and it goes haywire - stand still and let it recover.
